Frequently asked
How do I update my credit card?
Go to the Billing tab and click "Update payment method." Your card is processed securely through Stripe — we never see or store your full card number.
What's included in my Pro plan?
Your Pro plan includes: unlimited content updates (typically completed within 48 hours), monthly performance reports, daily backups, security monitoring, SSL renewal, plugin/CMS updates, priority message support, and up to 8 hours of design/dev work per month for new features.
How do I request a change to my site?
The fastest way is through the Requests tab — fill out the form with what you need and we'll get on it. For quick questions, the Messages tab is even faster. Both go to the same team.
How long do changes take?
Most content updates (text, photos, hours, contact info) are done within 24-48 hours. Design changes typically take 3-5 business days depending on scope. New features and larger projects get a custom timeline at kickoff.
Can I cancel my plan?
Yes, anytime. Pro and Starter plans are month-to-month — just message your team or email support@onvex.dev. We'll prorate any unused time and hand over all your assets and credentials before closing the account.
Who owns my website?
You do. You own your domain, content, design files, and the site itself. We don't lock you in — if you ever leave, you take everything with you.
How do I get a copy of my files?
Check the Files section on your site's detail page, or message us — we'll send a download link for backups, brand assets, and any files we have on record.
Didn't find your answer?
We're real humans, not bots. Reach out anytime.
